Our mission

Adoption changed where our stories continued. It did not erase where they began.

The Russian Adoptees Organization exists to create a lasting, credible home for adoptees with roots in Russia and the former Soviet Union.

Our goal is simple: make it easier to find one another, understand our histories, navigate complicated questions, preserve connection to our heritage, and build a community that can speak for itself.

What we do

A community built around the real needs of adoptees.

Not every adoptee wants the same thing. Our role is to create more paths, more information, and more connection.

About the organization

Independent. Adoptee-led. Built for the long term.

The Russian Adoptees Organization is an adoptee-led organization focused on community, practical support, heritage, education, and advocacy for people adopted from Russia and the former Soviet Union.

We are building an organization capable of maintaining useful resources, creating durable community infrastructure, developing relationships with relevant institutions, and ensuring adoptee perspectives remain at the center of our work.
