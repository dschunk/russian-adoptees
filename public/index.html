Our mission

Adoption changed where our stories continued. It did not erase where they began.

RAO exists to create a lasting, credible home for adoptees with roots in Russia and the former Soviet Union.

We make it easier to find one another, understand our histories, navigate citizenship and documentation questions, preserve cultural connection, share lived experience, and build a community capable of speaking for itself.

What we do

Built around the real needs of adoptees.

Not every adoptee wants the same relationship with identity, heritage, family, citizenship, or community. RAO creates more paths—not one prescribed answer.
