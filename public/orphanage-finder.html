Built for incomplete histories

Orphanage research is usually a clue-matching problem.

International adoptees often inherit records written at different times, translated by different people, and using names for institutions that later changed. The English word orphanage can hide several Russian terms, including дом ребёнка (baby home), детский дом (children's home), школа-интернат (boarding institution), or other organizations for children without parental care.

This tool does not guess. It organizes your evidence so RAO researchers can compare names, places, dates, institution types, staff names, court information, agency records, and historical terminology.

THE METHOD
  1. CaptureRecord every clue exactly as you know it.
  2. NormalizeSeparate places, people, dates, institutions, and source records.
  3. Cross-referenceBuild targeted Russian- and English-language research leads.
  4. VerifyDistinguish promising candidates from documented matches.
Please protect your identity documents.

Do not enter Social Security numbers, complete passport numbers, account credentials, or authentication codes. Do not paste full scans or entire adoption records into free-text fields. Names, dates, locations, institution clues, and short relevant wording are enough to start.

What RAO looks for
A single clue can connect to several record systems.
Researchers work outward from the strongest known facts rather than forcing every case into the same path.

      

        
01
Place
Historical city names, regions, districts, addresses, and nearby institutions can establish jurisdiction.

        
02
Institution
Numbers, translated names, Russian terminology, reorganizations, and successor organizations can narrow candidates.

        
03
People
Directors, doctors, caregivers, translators, and officials can connect otherwise ambiguous records.

        
04
Adoption trail
Courts, agencies, facilitators, guardianship bodies, and travel timelines can identify where source records should exist.

      

      
Find My Orphanage is a research-assistance service, not an official Russian government record search or identity-verification system. Historical institution names and administrative structures changed over time. RAO treats automatically generated search terms and possible institutions as leads until supported by documentary or other reliable evidence.
