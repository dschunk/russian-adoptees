Organization record

What RAO is building, adopting & working toward

A public timeline of major organization actions, policy milestones, platform decisions, and future announcements.

Organization timeline

August 29, 2026

Official contact, community, and visual identity systems go live

RAO activates its secure website inquiry system and official Press Office address, publishes the permanent Discord invitation, and introduces the navy-and-gold organization seal and favicon across the public website.

August 2026

New russianadoptees.com website launches on Cloudflare

RAO begins rebuilding its public website as a GitHub-backed, Cloudflare-hosted platform designed for long-term expansion, resource publishing, governance transparency, and community access.

October 2, 2025

Special Envoy role established

A presidential memorandum creates a Special Envoy role tasked with beginning formal dialogue with Russian diplomatic offices and reporting progress, challenges, and opportunities to RAO leadership.

September 17, 2025

Policy adopted for embassy and consular outreach

RAO establishes a policy of pursuing working relations with the Embassy of the Russian Federation and Russian consulates in support of adoptees navigating passport, visa, citizenship, legal, and documentation matters.

August 13, 2025

Discord formalized as official communication platform

RAO designates Discord as its central organizational and community communication platform, with public access and structured role-based permissions for staff and sensitive channels.

August 13, 2025

Official email standard adopted

RAO standardizes organization email accounts using the firstname.lastname@russianadoptees.com format and sets expectations for professional use and account security.

What comes next

The website is now being expanded into a public resource library, policy archive, community entry point, and citizenship/documentation guide. Future news posts will use this page for actual announcements rather than burying updates inside social-media feeds.

Next publishing phase

Original documents. Searchable guides. Clear public records.

RAO is preparing its memoranda and consular-reference materials for the public document archive, with context around what each document is, when it was issued, and whether it remains current.
