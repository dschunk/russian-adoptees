Privacy & data handling

Privacy at RAO

A plain-English explanation of the information RAO may receive through this website, why it is used, and the special care adoptees should take with sensitive records.

Effective August 29, 2026

Our approach to privacy

The Russian Adoptees Organization serves a community where identity, adoption history, citizenship, family-search information, government records, and personal stories can be unusually sensitive. Our default expectation is simple: collect less, ask for less, and tell people what not to send through ordinary web forms.

Information you choose to provide

When you use the RAO contact form, you may provide your name, email address, inquiry category, subject, and message. That information is used to route and respond to your inquiry, maintain appropriate organizational records, prevent abuse, and protect the integrity of RAO systems.

Do not send Social Security numbers, bank or payment information, passwords, authentication codes, complete passport numbers, or other credentials through the general contact form. If a future RAO process genuinely requires sensitive documentation, the organization should provide a more appropriate method rather than asking you to place it in an ordinary inquiry.

Website and security data

Like most websites, RAO's hosting and security providers may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as request metadata, IP addresses, browser information, security events, and diagnostic logs. RAO may use this information for reliability, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and security.

How information is used

  • To respond to inquiries and route them to the appropriate RAO function.
  • To manage volunteer, community, media, partnership, resource-correction, and organizational requests.
  • To maintain security, investigate abuse, and protect the website and community.
  • To preserve records when reasonably necessary for organizational continuity, accountability, or legal obligations.

Retention

RAO retains information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received, organizational recordkeeping, security, or applicable legal obligations. Different categories of records may require different retention periods. RAO does not publish a blanket promise that every message is deleted on a fixed schedule.

External services and community platforms

RAO links to external services such as Discord and Facebook. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and account practices. Information you provide directly to those platforms is governed by the platform, not by this website's privacy notice.

The website is hosted using Cloudflare infrastructure. Web fonts are currently requested from Google Fonts. Those providers may receive technical request information necessary to deliver their services.

Children and minors

RAO's public website may be viewed by younger adoptees, but the general contact form is not designed to collect sensitive information from children. Minors should use special care when sharing names at birth, exact dates of birth, adoption documents, location, family-search information, or government records, and should involve a trusted parent or guardian when appropriate.

Your choices

You may choose not to use the website contact form and instead contact RAO through an official email address. You may also ask RAO a reasonable question about information you previously submitted, including whether an inquiry can be corrected or deleted when no legal, security, or recordkeeping reason requires continued retention.

Changes to this notice

This notice may change as RAO's programs and technical systems evolve. Material updates should be reflected by a new effective date on this page.

Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to contact@russianadoptees.com or submitted through the official contact page.
