Start with the question you actually have.

Citizenship. Passports. Russian records. Birth family. Heritage. Return travel. Adoption paperwork. RAO is building the reference library we wish every Russian adoptee had been handed years ago.

Featured · Reviewed August 29, 2026

Were you adopted from Russia? You may still be a Russian citizen.

RAO reviewed the current Russian citizenship law, the historical laws that governed many international adoptions, current Foreign Ministry consular procedure, the 2026 citizenship-verification form changes, passport guidance, appointment systems, and the process for requesting records from Russia. The result is a plain-English, step-by-step guide written specifically for adoptees.

Our standard for high-stakes resources

RAO distinguishes between official government information, RAO's plain-English translation or explanation, and community experience. When a legal or consular rule matters, we aim to show you the primary source and the date we last checked it.

RAO resources are educational and community-oriented. Government procedures, laws, forms, fees, and travel conditions can change. Official determinations must come from the relevant authority.
