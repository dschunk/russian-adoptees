Executive leadership

The Administration

The Administration is responsible for carrying out the mission of the Russian Adoptees Organization, maintaining organizational standards, coordinating public resources and community programs, and representing adoptee interests with professionalism and accountability.

RAOEST. 2025

Office of the President

Executive leadership in service to the adoptee community.

The President directs the organization’s executive activities, oversees implementation of adopted policy, coordinates institutional relationships, and ensures the organization remains focused on the needs and voices of Russian and former-Soviet adoptees.

EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP
DMS

President

David M. Schunk

President & Founder · Russian Adoptees Organization

As President, David M. Schunk provides executive direction for the organization and its public mission: connecting Russian adoptees, improving access to practical information, preserving cultural and personal heritage, strengthening community infrastructure, and advocating on issues that affect adoptees.

The Office of the President also oversees organizational policy implementation and, until responsibilities are delegated to permanent officers or departments, may coordinate external institutional and consular outreach on behalf of the organization.

Organizational structure

Administration functions

RAO is building a durable leadership structure around clear responsibility, documented policy, community service, and accountable use of organizational authority.

01

Executive Direction

Sets organizational priorities, implements policy, coordinates leadership, and maintains continuity of the mission.

02

Community Engagement

Supports adoptee-only community spaces, regional connections, meetups, volunteers, and member communication.

03

Resources & Advocacy

Develops practical adoptee guidance, tracks relevant policy changes, and elevates issues affecting Russian adoptees.

04

External Relations

Coordinates appropriate outreach to institutions, cultural organizations, diplomatic offices, and other relevant stakeholders.

05

Communications

Maintains official announcements, public information, digital platforms, organizational documents, and media-facing standards.

06

Governance & Accountability

Maintains documented rules, role boundaries, security practices, public policies, and internal standards for responsible operation.

Public accountability
