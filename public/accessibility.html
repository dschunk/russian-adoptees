Accessibility

An accessible public record matters.

RAO is committed to making its community information, official resources, public documents, and contact channels usable across devices and assistive technologies.

Effective August 29, 2026

Accessibility commitment

The Russian Adoptees Organization wants its public website, resources, policies, and contact paths to be usable by as many people as reasonably possible. RAO aims to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA as the site evolves.

Current design practices

  • Keyboard-accessible navigation, links, buttons, and forms.
  • A visible skip-to-content link for keyboard and assistive-technology users.
  • Responsive layouts intended to remain usable on phones, tablets, and large displays.
  • Text and controls designed for strong contrast against their backgrounds.
  • Visible focus indicators for interactive elements.
  • Reduced-motion behavior when a visitor's operating system requests it.
  • Semantic headings, labels, landmarks, and descriptive page titles.
  • Print-friendly treatment for long reference pages and public documents.

Zoom, text size, and reflow

The site is designed to tolerate browser zoom and narrower viewports without requiring a desktop-sized display. Visitors may use browser zoom, operating-system text scaling, reader features, or other assistive tools according to their needs.

Documents and source material

RAO increasingly publishes web-readable versions of policies and reference material because HTML is generally easier to search, resize, navigate, and use with assistive technology than a scanned document. When original PDFs are made available, RAO should continue to provide an accessible web version or equivalent context whenever practical.

Known limitations

Accessibility is an ongoing engineering and publishing responsibility, not a one-time certification. External sites linked by RAO—including government websites, social platforms, and third-party documents—may have accessibility limitations outside RAO's control.

Report a barrier

If you encounter an accessibility problem, please tell us what page you were using, what went wrong, and—if you are comfortable sharing it—what browser, device, or assistive technology you were using. That information can help reproduce and correct the problem.

Use the official contact form or email contact@russianadoptees.com.
