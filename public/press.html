Media standard

RAO distinguishes official organizational statements from personal opinions expressed by individual adoptees or community members. When reporting an RAO position, journalists should rely on material published on russianadoptees.com or confirmation from an authorized organization representative.

Organization at a glance

Russian Adoptees Organization

Background information suitable for media, partner organizations, researchers, and public reference.

01

Who we serve

People adopted from Russia and former-Soviet countries, with an emphasis on adoptee-led community, practical resources, heritage, identity, and advocacy.

02

What we publish

Adoptee-specific guides, official-source translations, organizational policies, public memoranda, community information, and updates affecting Russian adoptees.

03

Community

RAO builds on an established adoptee-only Facebook community and is developing additional structured communication and regional infrastructure.

04

Citizenship resources

RAO translates and explains official Russian citizenship and consular information while making clear that only government authorities can determine an individual’s legal status.

05

Governance

The organization publishes major internal policies and formal documents so members can understand leadership decisions and organizational standards.

06

Independence

RAO is an independent adoptee-led organization and is not a government agency, embassy, consulate, diplomatic mission, or subdivision of any government.
