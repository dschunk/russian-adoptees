An adoptee-only space

The Facebook group is intentionally reserved for adoptees. Adoption carries experiences around identity, grief, family, belonging, trauma, culture, citizenship, and reunion that can be deeply personal. The purpose of an adoptee-only environment is not to diminish adoptive families; it is to give adopted people room to speak candidly with one another without feeling that they need to defend, soften, or explain their experiences.

Parents, researchers, media, agencies, service providers, and other non-adoptees should use RAO's public website and official contact channels rather than attempting to enter private adoptee spaces.

Community safety comes first

Privacy

Protect identifying information

You control what parts of your story you share. Names at birth, dates of birth, photographs, adoption documents, orphanage records, and biological-family information can be highly sensitive. Share them only when you understand the privacy risk.

Never publish

Keep critical identifiers private

Never post Social Security numbers, bank or financial information, complete passport numbers, passwords, authentication codes, or other credentials. When sharing a document for help, redact information that another person does not need to see.

Young adoptees

Extra protection for minors

Children under 13 should have a parent or guardian's approval before attempting to join community spaces and should not share personal identifying information. Older minors should also use special care when discussing records, family-search information, location, or contact details.

Respect

Zero tolerance for bullying

Bullying, harassment, inappropriate attacks, targeted negativity, and unnecessary drama are not acceptable. Moderators may warn members and remove people who continue behavior that harms the community.

How RAO's Discord is designed to work

Under the organization's adopted communications policy, Discord serves as the central hub for announcements, discussion, coordination, resource sharing, staff activity, and community engagement.

Public doors. Structured access.

The server is publicly joinable for community outreach, while administrative, moderator, staff, and sensitive channels remain restricted to authorized roles.

Public community

Accessible entry point

Adoptees can join the server, find organization announcements, participate in community spaces, and connect with others without receiving access to internal administrative areas.

Role-based access

Clear permissions

Administrative, moderator, staff, and sensitive channels are restricted based on assigned roles. Access is intended to match responsibility rather than simply server membership.

Security & moderation

Protected infrastructure

The policy calls for deliberate permission configuration, moderation tooling, protected invite-only discussions where appropriate, and technical oversight of the server.

Official information

One place for RAO updates

Organization leadership and staff are expected to maintain an active presence so members have a consistent place to receive updates, resources, and responses.

What the community is building

  • Regional channels and representatives that help adoptees find one another locally.
  • In-person and virtual meetups around the United States and, as the community grows, internationally.
  • Citizenship, passport, translation, documentation, Russian-record, and travel resources.
  • Biological-family search knowledge and peer experience.
  • Spaces for heritage, language, identity, Russian culture, stories, and everyday conversation.
  • Organization announcements, volunteer opportunities, and public policy updates.

Community administration

The Russian adoptee community has long relied on active administrators and a committee structure to keep discussions usable and safe. RAO continues that work through its leadership, volunteers, moderators, technical staff, and regional representatives. Members should contact the administration whenever they have a safety concern, moderation issue, question about organization activity, or idea for a meetup.

Community participation is voluntary. RAO cannot guarantee the identity, intentions, or accuracy of information shared by individual members. Protect your personal information, verify sensitive claims independently, and use additional caution in biological-family searches, financial requests, document exchanges, and off-platform communications.
