Official community platform

A place to actually know one another.

RAO’s community infrastructure is designed to be open enough for adoptees to find us, while keeping staff, moderation, and sensitive conversations properly separated and protected.

Discord is RAO’s official communication platform.

Under the organization’s adopted communications policy, Discord serves as the central hub for announcements, discussion, coordination, resource sharing, staff activity, and community engagement.

Community principle

Public doors. Structured access.

The server is intended to be publicly joinable for community outreach, while administrative, moderator, and sensitive channels remain restricted to authorized roles.

How the server is intended to work

Public community

Accessible entry point

Adoptees can join the server, find organization announcements, participate in community spaces, and connect with others without receiving access to internal administrative areas.

Role-based access

Clear permissions

Administrative, moderator, staff, and sensitive channels are restricted based on assigned roles. Access is intended to match responsibility rather than simply server membership.

Security & moderation

Protected community infrastructure

The policy calls for deliberate permission configuration, moderation tooling, protected invite-only discussions where appropriate, and technical oversight of the server.

Official information

A central source for RAO updates

Organization leadership and staff are expected to maintain an active presence so members have a consistent place to receive updates, resources, and responses.

What we are building inside the community

  • Regional channels that help adoptees connect locally and organize meetups.
  • Citizenship, passport, translation, documentation, and travel resource discussions.
  • Organization announcements and official policy updates.
  • Volunteer and staff coordination areas.
  • Spaces for heritage, language, identity, stories, and everyday conversation.

Community expectations

RAO’s communication policy is built around an open but orderly environment. Members are expected to respect role boundaries, moderation decisions, privacy, and other adoptees. Sensitive discussions should stay in the channels designated for them.

As the community grows, server rules, onboarding instructions, role descriptions, and moderation standards will be published here alongside the Discord invite.
