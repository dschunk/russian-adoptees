Before you write

Help us route your inquiry.

Including a clear subject and the type of help you need makes it easier to respond appropriately.

01

Citizenship

Tell us whether you are asking about status verification, an old Russian passport, or a current passport process.

02

Documents

Describe the type of record you are trying to locate—birth, adoption, court, archive, or other documentation.

03

Community

Use this for Facebook, Discord, meetups, moderation concerns, or connecting with other Russian adoptees.

04

Official inquiries

Media, organizational, partnership, institutional, volunteer, and formal administrative inquiries are welcome.

Privacy reminder

RAO encourages adoptees to protect sensitive personal information. Do not send Social Security numbers, bank information, passwords, authentication codes, or complete passport numbers. If a future process requires sensitive documentation, RAO should provide a more appropriate secure method rather than asking you to place it in a general inquiry.
