Memorandum · August 13, 2025

Official Communication Platform — Discord

To: All Members, Volunteers, and Interested Parties

From: Executive Board

Effective immediately, the Russian Adoptees Organization will adopt Discord as our official communication platform.

Purpose

Discord will serve as our central hub for announcements, discussions, coordination, and community engagement. This decision allows us to maintain a structured, secure, and accessible communication environment for all members, while enabling broad participation from our global community.

Access & Roles

  • The Discord server will be publicly joinable for community outreach.
  • Role-based permissions will be strictly enforced to ensure security and orderly communication.
  • Access to administrative, moderator, and sensitive discussion channels will be restricted to authorized personnel only.
  • Clear role definitions and responsibilities will be published for transparency.

Security & Permissions

  • Permissions will be configured to prevent unauthorized access or misuse of server resources.
  • Moderation tools will be configured to detect and manage violations of community guidelines.
  • Sensitive discussions will take place in secured, invite-only channels.

Implementation

An IT and security lead will oversee technical setup, permissions configuration, and ongoing security monitoring. David Schunk volunteered to serve in this capacity.

Next Steps

  • Server rules, role descriptions, and onboarding instructions will be published prior to full launch.
  • All members will be notified once the server is officially open.

The Executive Board expects all members to adhere to established rules and respect role boundaries. This system is designed to promote an open yet orderly environment, safeguard member privacy, and preserve the integrity of our communications.

Memorandum · August 13, 2025

Standardized Email Address Format

To: All Members, Volunteers, and Staff

From: Executive Board

Effective immediately, all official email accounts issued by the Russian Adoptees Organization will follow a standardized naming format to ensure consistency, professionalism, and ease of communication.

Email Address Format

firstname.lastname@russianadoptees.com

Guidelines

  1. First Name / Last Name: Use the legal first and last name registered with the organization. Names are lowercase and separated by a period.
  2. Special Characters: Hyphens and apostrophes will be removed or replaced with appropriate characters.
  3. Account Purpose: Addresses are for official organization business. External communications representing RAO must use the official address.
  4. Access & Security: Accounts are provisioned by the IT Department. Password, multifactor authentication, and security requirements must be followed. Accounts are disabled when a role ends.

This standardized format ensures communications remain professional, recognizable, and secure.

Presidential Memorandum · September 17, 2025

Establishing Relations with the Russian Embassy and Consulates

To: All Members of the Administration

From: David M. Schunk, President

In pursuit of our mission to advocate for adoptees of Russian and former Soviet origin, this memorandum establishes the following policy:

1. Diplomatic Relations

The Russian Adoptees Organization will actively establish and maintain working relations with the Embassy of the Russian Federation in Washington, D.C., as well as Russian consulates across the United States and abroad.

2. Purpose

These relations shall serve to:

  • Assist adoptees with passport, visa, and citizenship-related matters.
  • Address ongoing challenges and legal issues adoptees face with their countries of origin.
  • Promote cooperation and mutual understanding between adoptees and Russian institutions.

3. Responsibilities

Until a permanent department is established, communications and coordination with the Russian Embassy and consulates fall under the Office of the President, with oversight and delegation as necessary.

4. Commitment

The Organization reaffirms its duty to provide adoptees with access to resources, representation, and support in navigating embassy and consular procedures.

This memorandum is effective immediately and shall guide all related actions moving forward.

David M. Schunk
President
Russian Adoptees Organization

Memorandum · October 2, 2025

Establishment of Special Envoy to Russian Embassy & Consulates

To: Leadership Team and Advisors

From: Office of the President

Purpose

In order to advance the mission of the Russian Adoptees Organization and address challenges involving citizenship, passports, and legal recognition, this memorandum establishes a Special Envoy role tasked with beginning formal dialogue with the Russian Federation’s Embassy and Consulates.

Appointment & Duties

The Special Envoy shall:

  • Serve as the primary liaison between the Organization and Russian diplomatic offices in the United States.
  • Initiate outreach to introduce RAO’s mission and objectives.
  • Facilitate discussions regarding citizenship, passport applications, documentation access, consular support, and other issues affecting adoptees.
  • Provide regular reports to the President and Leadership Team regarding progress, challenges, and opportunities for collaboration.

Authority

The Special Envoy acts under the direction of the President and in consultation with the Leadership Team. The position does not hold independent negotiating authority but serves to establish relationships and open communication channels.

Next Steps

  1. Formal selection and appointment of the Special Envoy.
  2. Drafting of an introductory letter to the Russian Embassy and relevant Consulates.
  3. Scheduling of initial meetings or calls to introduce the Organization and open dialogue.

Organization Policy

Policy on Supporting Adoptees

Purpose

This policy establishes RAO’s commitment to advocate for, support, and empower adoptees of Russian and former Soviet origin. It provides guiding principles and actions to ensure adoptees have access to resources, representation, and a strong community.

Scope

This policy applies to all members, volunteers, and leadership of RAO and guides interactions with adoptees, government bodies, embassies and consulates, and partner organizations.

1. Advocacy for Rights

  • Champion adoptee rights, including recognition of citizenship, access to legal documentation, and fair treatment under the law.
  • Engage with governments, agencies, and institutions to address systemic challenges facing adoptees.

2. Consular & Embassy Support

  • Develop and maintain working relationships with the Russian Embassy and consulates, as well as other relevant institutions.
  • Assist adoptees in navigating passport, visa, and citizenship issues.

3. Community Empowerment

  • Foster a strong global community through regional representatives, meetups, digital platforms, and peer support networks.
  • Prioritize mental health and well-being by supporting safe spaces for adoptees to share experiences.

4. Cultural Preservation

  • Encourage adoptees to explore and embrace heritage through cultural education, language resources, and historical awareness.
  • Pursue partnerships with cultural organizations for learning and exchange.

5. Transparency & Accountability

  • Maintain open communication with members and responsibly manage organizational resources.
  • Establish oversight to protect the integrity of programs and initiatives.

Implementation

  • The President and Vice President oversee rollout.
  • Regional representatives and volunteers are empowered to support adoptees in their territories.
  • External partnerships may be developed to expand services and resources.

Review

The policy shall be reviewed annually and updated as necessary to reflect evolving adoptee needs and organizational growth.

Approved by: David M. Schunk, President
