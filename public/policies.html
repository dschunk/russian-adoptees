Public record

Policies, memoranda & organizational direction

RAO is building a public institutional record so members can see what the organization has formally adopted, why those decisions were made, and how they guide our work.

Core policy commitments

The Policy on Supporting Adoptees establishes RAO’s broad framework: advocate for adoptee rights, build consular and embassy support, strengthen community, preserve cultural connection, and operate with transparency and accountability.

Organization policy

Supporting adoptees

RAO commits to championing citizenship recognition, legal documentation access, and fair treatment; developing relevant institutional relationships; connecting adoptees through regional and digital networks; supporting cultural education and language; and maintaining accountable leadership.

Official memoranda

August 13, 2025

Official Communication Platform — Discord

Discord is designated as the organization’s central communication hub. The policy calls for public community access with role-based restrictions for administrative, moderator, and sensitive channels, plus formal oversight of permissions and security.

August 13, 2025

Standardized Email Address Format

Official RAO email accounts use the format firstname.lastname@russianadoptees.com. The memorandum also requires organization business to use official accounts and establishes security expectations around account provisioning and access.

September 17, 2025

Establishing Relations with the Russian Embassy and Consulates

RAO adopts a policy of establishing and maintaining working relations with Russian diplomatic and consular offices in support of adoptees facing passport, visa, citizenship, documentation, and related issues. The policy also emphasizes cooperation and access to consular resources.

October 2, 2025

Establishment of Special Envoy

RAO creates a Special Envoy role to serve as a primary liaison with Russian diplomatic offices, begin formal outreach, facilitate discussions about issues affecting adoptees, and report progress to organizational leadership. The memorandum makes clear that the envoy acts under organizational direction and does not hold independent negotiating authority.

What these policies do not claim

RAO’s memoranda establish the organization’s internal policy and intended outreach. They do not, by themselves, establish an official affiliation, endorsement, partnership, or governmental authority with the Russian Federation, the United States, any embassy, or any consulate.

Why publish this?

Policy is useful only when members can actually see it. As the organization grows, this page will become a public archive of major governance decisions, revisions, and institutional priorities.

Future additions will include original downloadable PDFs, revision history, adoption dates, responsible offices or officers, and superseded-policy notices where appropriate.
